Our Story

Kalinga Kitchen & Bar is your neighbourhood restaurant where great taste meets warm hospitality. For years, we've been serving Rajamahendravaram with a simple mission: bring together the finest flavours from across India and beyond, in a space where families, friends, and colleagues feel genuinely welcome.

Every dish on our menu is crafted with care – from our signature biryanis and Andhra specialities to our Chinese favourites and bar snacks. We believe that food is more than just sustenance; it's an experience, a memory, a reason to gather.

Our team takes pride in consistent quality, proper hygiene, and hospitality that makes you feel at home. Whether you're here for a family dinner, a friend's celebration, or a casual evening out – you'll find food worth savouring and a welcome worth remembering.
